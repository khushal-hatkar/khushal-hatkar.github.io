This is my ongoing master's thesis, building on prior work from the lab on In₂Se₃'s layered ferroelectric polymorphs. I'm using in-situ heating experiments inside the TEM to track heat-assisted phase transitions in 3R In₂Se₃, alongside in-situ electrical biasing to directly image ferroelectric switching and polarisation vorticity as they occur - rather than inferring switching behaviour indirectly. The goal is to connect the local atomic-scale structural changes to the macroscopic ferroelectric response.
