As part of a short research project in Prof. Praveen Kumar's lab, my main contribution was developing a reliable polishing protocol for tin - a genuinely difficult material to prepare for EBSD given its softness and low melting point, which I worked out on my own. Once I had EBSD-quality surfaces, I used EBSD orientation mapping to characterise grain orientations and grain-boundary character distributions, and verified composition and microstructure via SEM, EDS, and WDS on the Helios G4-UX and Apreo 2S. I transitioned to a different lab before the project extended further.
